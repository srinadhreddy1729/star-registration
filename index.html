PERSONAL STAR DEDICATION
GOOD TO KNOW

A few things about your star.

Is this an official astronomical registration?

No. This is a symbolic dedication in an artistic virtual galaxy, not ownership or an official name for a real star.

Where are my stars saved?

Registrations are saved in this browser on this device. They are not synced between devices; clearing browser storage removes them. Download your certificate to keep a copy.

How do I find my star?

Open My stars and click its ID, or enter the exact ID or name in Explore. The camera flies to your star and opens its details.

Can I download a certificate?

Yes. Open a star’s details and tap Download certificate to save a high-resolution PNG with the full dedication.
